# Roadmap

## MVP / v1 included
- Auth/profile and household membership.
- Students, schedules, lessons, prep notes, online/in-person, Google Calendar/Meet adapter.
- Lesson completion → receivable → confirmed payment allocation.
- Accounts, manual transactions, CSV import, mock bank sync, transaction matching.
- Full subscription hierarchy and planned occurrence/payment matching.
- Household inventory, shopping, cleaning, shared expenses, exact splits, settlement.
- Grow surplus calculation, safety reserve, contribution plan, transfer tracking.
- Home dashboard, core analytics, responsive PWA, accessibility, export/delete.

## Integration graduation path
1. Mock providers and fixtures.
2. Local/preview app with manual/CSV finance data.
3. Google Calendar OAuth in a development Google Cloud project.
4. KFTC developer testbed adapter.
5. Formal KFTC application/contract/security review.
6. Production read-only activation behind a feature flag and operational approval.

## Explicit backlog
- Receipt OCR.
- Barcode scanning.
- AI-assisted categorization with explanation/confirmation.
- AI monthly report.
- Push/email reminders.
- Broader budgeting and fixed recurring rules.
- Rich card automation and broader asset allocation.
- Any money movement or brokerage action, which requires a separate safety/legal redesign.
