# Product Goals

## Product statement
Student OS is a Korean personal operations app for a university student who earns steady tutoring income, manages private bank balances and spending, tracks monthly subscriptions, allocates true surplus to safety cash and long-term contributions, and shares a two-room home with one friend.

## Primary outcome
Opening one app should answer four questions quickly:

1. What tutoring work must I prepare for today?
2. What money was actually received, spent, or is still receivable?
3. What household stock, cleaning, and roommate settlement needs attention?
4. After upcoming obligations, how much money is genuinely available to reserve or invest?

## Success principles

- **Action before analytics:** today's lesson prep, due payment, renewal, cleaning, low stock, and settlement appear before decorative charts.
- **No accounting fiction:** work, receivables, planned bills, transactions, transfers, and shared responsibility are distinct.
- **Fast manual fallback:** every external integration has a complete mock/manual workflow.
- **Shared without oversharing:** roommate collaboration never reveals private accounts or private ledger entries.
- **Student-scale investing:** track disciplined surplus allocation, not trading.
- **Trustworthy automation:** matching is suggested with evidence and confirmed by the user.

## Non-goals for MVP

- Open-banking transfers, bill payment, brokerage execution, stock recommendations, tax filing, receipt OCR, barcode scanning, automatic AI categorization, or an enterprise accounting system.
- Storing full school-record documents or unnecessary sensitive student information.
- Pretending production KFTC connectivity is available before formal onboarding.
